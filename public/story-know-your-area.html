Safety tip

Knowing your area before you set out

4 min read

The safest choice is often the one you make before you leave the house. FlagRisk puts what is happening around you in one place so that choice is easy.

One map of what has been reported

FlagRisk draws the safety concerns reported near you, from the people around you and from published news, onto one map, with a quick Risk Score for any area.

Follow the places that matter

Your home, your route, a child's school. Follow a place and FlagRisk tells you when a concern is reported near it, so you are not caught out.

Choose the route or the time

If the map shows something on your usual way, a different road or a different hour is a small change that makes a real difference.

Flag what you see

When you report a concern, it shows on the map without your name, so other people can choose better too. The area gets safer when everyone adds what they know.
