The short version

We hold where you are, where you travel, and who you would call for help. That is unusually sensitive, and we treat that information accordingly.

Your location is shared with the people you choose, and with nobody else. We never sell personal data. Nothing we publish, license, or sell to any organisation identifies you or any other person, at any price, on any plan.

What we build a business on is the pattern, never the person.

4.Who can see your location

4.1

The people in your trusted circle see your location when you raise a panic alert, and during a declared journey where that feature is active. You choose those people and you can remove them at any time.

4.2

Other users of FlagRisk never see your location, your identity, or your movements. A report you submit appears on the map without your name.

4.3

Our staff may access your data only where access is necessary for a specific task, such as investigating a fault or moderating a report. Access is logged. Our staff are contractually prohibited from looking up any person for personal reasons or on behalf of a third party, and breach is a dismissal matter.

4.4

We do not permit any organisation to locate, track, or identify an individual user through our products.

4.5

An alert delivered on WhatsApp carries one position, with its source and its age. We show no moving trail of a person on WhatsApp.

4.6

If you choose to tell your trusted circle about a report you have made, the members you tell see your name, the kind of report, and where you made it. A person who is asked whether a reported incident is still happening is never told who reported it.

4.7

A person you invite to your trusted circle sees your name. A person who receives a note you send through Tell a friend sees your name, because you send that note from your own WhatsApp.

4.8

You are shown which members of your trusted circle have marked a panic alert as seen.

4.9

The places you follow, and the names you give them, are shown to you alone. No member of your trusted circle and no other user sees them. Following a place does not share your position with anyone.

5.Aggregate information and our business model

5.1

We combine reports and incident data to produce aggregate information on safety concerns, such as the level of reported safety concerns in an area by street, hour, and season. That is the basis of the products we sell to organisations.

5.2

Aggregate information is stripped of names, mobile numbers, account identifiers, and device identifiers, and is generalised so that an individual cannot reasonably be singled out.

5.3

We do not sell personal data. We do not license, publish, or otherwise make available to any customer any data that identifies a user, and no plan or price tier changes that.

5.4

A photograph attached to a report is checked by an automated service for relevance to the report before the photograph is shown to other people. We do not use your data to make an automated decision that produces a legal effect for you or that significantly affects you.

7.Requests from law enforcement and authorities

7.1

We disclose personal data to a law enforcement agency or other authority only where we are compelled to do so by a valid legal instrument, such as a court order, or where disclosure is necessary to protect the vital interests of a person facing an immediate danger of serious harm.

7.2

We do not give any agency standing or bulk access to our systems, and we do not respond to informal requests.

7.3

We record every request we receive and the response we give.

7.4

We tell you if we have disclosed your data, unless we are legally prohibited from doing so or telling you would put a person in danger.

8.Where your data is held

8.1

Our infrastructure providers hold data on servers located outside Nigeria.

8.2

We transfer personal data outside Nigeria only where the Act permits the transfer, which for us means under a written contract imposing protection equivalent to that required by the Act, or to a country recognised as providing an adequate level of protection.

8.3

You may ask us for the current list of the countries in which your data is held.

10.Security

10.1

We protect data in transit and at rest using encryption, restrict access to our systems by role, require multi-factor authentication for administrative access, and log access to user data.

10.2

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach occurs which is likely to result in a risk to your rights and freedoms, we notify the Nigeria Data Protection Commission within seventy-two hours where the Act requires notification, and we tell you without undue delay where the risk to you is high.

10.3

Keep your device locked and your account credentials private. Tell us immediately if you think someone else has access to your account.

11.Your rights

Under the Act you have the right to:

  • be informed about how we use your data, which is the purpose of this policy;
  • ask for a copy of the personal data we hold about you;
  • have inaccurate data corrected;
  • ask us to delete your data, where we have no overriding reason to keep that data;
  • ask us to restrict how we use your data while a concern is resolved;
  • object to processing we carry out on the basis of our legitimate interests;
  • receive your data in a portable form, or have your data sent to another provider;
  • withdraw consent at any time, where we rely on consent; and
  • complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.
11.1

To exercise a right, write to the data protection address in clause 14. We respond within one month, and we tell you if we need longer because the request is complex.

11.2

We do not charge for this, unless a request is manifestly unfounded or excessive.

11.3

You can delete your account in the Service at any time, which starts the deletion described in clause 9. A person who uses FlagRisk only on WhatsApp asks us to delete their account using the form on our Delete your account and data page. We confirm that it is you before we act.

11.4

You withdraw your consent to alerts on WhatsApp by sending STOP ALERTS to the FlagRisk account on WhatsApp. Sending START ALERTS turns those alerts on again. We record each choice with its time. Choosing to follow a place on WhatsApp turns alerts on there, unless you have stopped them. STOP ALERTS also stops the alerts on WhatsApp about the places you follow.

11.5

You stop following a place at any time from My places in the FlagRisk chat on WhatsApp. That place is then deleted.

12.Children

12.1

The Service is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

12.2

If we learn that we hold data of a person under 18, we delete that data and close the account.

13.Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We publish the revised version with a new effective date and, where a change materially affects you, we tell you before the change takes effect.
