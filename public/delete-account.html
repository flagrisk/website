The short version

You can delete your FlagRisk account, and the personal data we hold about you, at any time. The fastest way is in the app. If you cannot use the app, use the request form on this page, and we confirm that it is you before we act.

Request account deletion

1.How to delete your account

1.1

In the FlagRisk app, open Settings, then Account, then Delete account. We ask you to confirm, and then we delete your account straight away. This cannot be undone.

1.2

If you cannot use the app, fill in the request form on this page. It reaches our data protection team directly, and we work it as a formal deletion request.

1.3

Before we delete anything, we confirm that it is you. We send a one time code to the mobile number on the account. We do this so that nobody else can delete your account, and with it your trusted circle and your safety history. We do not act on a request that we cannot confirm.

1.4

If you use FlagRisk only on WhatsApp, your WhatsApp number and your account are the same account. Use the request form on this page, with that WhatsApp number as your mobile number. To stop alerts without deleting your account, send STOP ALERTS to the FlagRisk account on WhatsApp.

1.5

We reply to every request within one month, and we tell you if a request is complex and needs longer. Closing the account stops FlagRisk alerts, including alerts on WhatsApp, and removes you from any trusted circle you belong to.

Request account deletion

Use this form to ask us to delete your FlagRisk account and your personal data. We confirm that it is you before we act, so give the mobile number on your account.

Request received

Thank you. Your deletion request has reached our data protection team. We confirm that it is you, then delete your account and personal data, and we reply within one month.

2.What we delete

These items are removed when your account is closed.

DataWhat happens
Account and profileName, mobile number, email address, password, profile photo and verification status. Deleted when we close your account.
Trusted circleThe names and mobile numbers of the people you added, and your own connections to other circles. Removed with your account.
Followed and saved placesThe places you follow or save, with the names you gave them. Removed with your account.
Location historyRemoved with your account, and in any case never kept longer than the period your plan provides, being 7, 30, 90 or 180 days.
Photographs and videoKept while your account is open, and deleted when we close your account, unless we must hold an item for a moderation or legal matter.
Alerts and journeysPanic alerts, check-ins and Trip Watch records are deleted when we close your account.
WhatsApp message recordsThe record of messages exchanged, which holds no message text, is deleted after twelve months. The private words you set for a panic alert on WhatsApp are deleted when the account is closed.
Device and usage dataNotification token, device details and usage records linked to your account are removed with it.

3.What we keep, and for how long

Some data stays after your account is closed, for the reasons set out below.

DataWhy and for how long
Billing recordsPlan, billing history and payment reference. Kept for six years, to meet tax and accounting obligations. We never hold your card details.
Reports you submittedA report forms part of the safety record that other people rely on. When we close your account it is de-identified from you, so it no longer shows who made it, and it stays on the map record.
Records we must keep by lawIf a law, a court order or a regulator requires us to hold data, we keep it for the required period and no longer.
Aggregate safety informationCounts and patterns by area, hour and season. It has no names, numbers or identifiers, so it does not contain personal data and is kept.

The full retention schedule is in section 9 of our Privacy Policy.

4.Other requests about your data

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 you can also ask for a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, object to how we use it, or withdraw consent. Use the same email address, and tell us what you need.

If you are not happy with our response, you may complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission. We would rather hear from you first, and we will try to put the matter right.
