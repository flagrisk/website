For families

One circle for the whole family.

Children heading out, elders on a basic phone, everyone in different places. FlagRisk holds the whole family in one circle, whatever phone each person carries.

Create your accountUse on WhatsApp
Any phone

No one is left out for want of a smartphone.

FlagRisk runs on one system behind four ways in: the app, WhatsApp, SMS and USSD. A parent on an old handset uses a short code with no internet, a teenager uses the app, and both are in the same family circle, reaching the same people.

  • App for Android and iPhone
  • WhatsApp, with nothing to install
  • SMS for any phone with signal
  • USSD short code, no internet needed
Know your area

Watch the places that matter to your family.

Follow your home, a route, a child's school. FlagRisk tells you when a concern is reported near one of them, drawn from people nearby and from published news, so you hear of it early rather than after.

  • Follow home, school and daily routes
  • Told when a concern is reported near them
  • A live map with a Risk Score for any area
  • Flag a concern for the neighbourhood, anonymously
Raise an alarm

Reach each other in one tap.

If anything feels wrong, one press tells the family circle at once, with a live location. On a paid plan, people who cannot be reached by a notification are reached by SMS, so the message gets through.

  • One press reaches the family circle
  • Live location sent the moment it fires
  • You see who has acknowledged
  • SMS backup to your circle on a paid plan
