For families

A family safety app for every phone.

Children heading out, elders on a basic handset, everyone in different places. FlagRisk holds the whole family in one circle, whatever phone each person carries.

Create your accountUse on WhatsApp

A family safety app only works if it leaves no one out. In Nigeria that means working on the newest smartphone and the oldest feature phone alike. FlagRisk runs on one system behind four ways in, so a parent on USSD and a teenager on the app share the same family circle and reach the same people.

For the whole family

One circle that holds everyone.

Everyone in one circle

Parents, children and grandparents in a single family circle. A grandparent on a basic phone uses a short code; a teenager uses the app. Both are covered.

Watch the places that matter

Follow your home, a route, a child's school. FlagRisk tells you when a concern is reported near one of them, drawn from people nearby and from published news.

Reach each other in one tap

If anything feels wrong, one press tells the family circle at once, with a live location. On a paid plan, people who miss the notification are reached by SMS.

One brain, every phone

No one left out for want of a smartphone.

FlagRisk works across the app, WhatsApp, SMS and USSD, so the whole family is reachable whether or not they have data.
