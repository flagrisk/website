Our story

Built for the walk home.

FlagRisk started with a simple belief: safety should not depend on a stranger picking up the phone. It should start with the people who already love you.

Across Nigeria, too many people face a frightening moment with no quick way to reach the people who could help.

FlagRisk turns the people you trust into the help that reaches you first. Instead of calling a stranger who cannot see where you are and may arrive too late, you reach a circle of family and friends in one tap, with your live location, so help is personal and it is close.

We built FlagRisk to work for everyone, on any phone. One backend serves four channels, the app, WhatsApp, SMS and USSD, so your circle can include the people who matter, whatever phone they carry. Safety should not be a privilege of the newest device.

What we stand for

The principles behind FlagRisk.

People first

Help that comes from someone who loves you, not a faceless line. Your circle is the heart of everything we build.

Privacy by design

Your location is shared only with the people you choose, only when you choose. It is never sold or made public.

Built for Africa

Designed for real streets, real networks and every kind of phone, so no one is left outside the circle.
