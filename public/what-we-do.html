What we do

The whole of your safety, in one circle.

Three things, working quietly together: raise an alarm, share a trip, and know your area. Here is how each one reaches the people who love you.

Create your accountUse on WhatsApp
Raise an alarm

One tap reaches the people who love you.

No call to make and no stranger to convince. One press sends your live location to your circle, so the people who can actually reach you know at once, even if you cannot speak.

  • A silent alarm that raises no sound and shows nothing on screen
  • Your live location shared the moment it fires
  • It reaches your circle first, not a faceless line
Share a trip

Watched all the way to your door.

Share a journey and FlagRisk checks you in along the way. If you go quiet before you arrive, your circle is told where you were last seen, without you lifting a finger.

  • Set your destination and who is watching
  • Automatic check-ins as you travel
  • Your circle alerted if you do not arrive
Know your area

See what is happening around you.

Verified safety concerns reported near you, as they happen, so you can choose a different route or a different time before you set out, rather than finding out after.

  • A live map of concerns reported nearby
  • A quick Risk Score for any area
  • Flag a concern so others can choose better too

One brain, every phone

The same safety, whatever phone your people carry.

FlagRisk runs on one system behind four ways in, so no one in your circle is left out for want of a smartphone or data.

App

Android and iPhone

The full experience. Alarms, Trip Watch, the live map and your circle, all in one place.

WhatsApp

Right inside a chat

Start and use FlagRisk in a WhatsApp conversation. Raise an alarm, check an area, and bring people in, with nothing to install.

SMS

Any phone with signal

Trigger an alarm and reach your circle by text when data is not an option.

USSD

No internet needed

Use a short code to reach help on the most basic handset.
