For students

Safety for students, far from home.

A new city, late classes, a long trip back for the holidays. FlagRisk puts family back home and friends nearby in one circle, so someone is always close enough to reach you.

Create your accountUse on WhatsApp
Your circle

One circle, from home to campus.

Add the people you trust whatever phone they carry: family back home on a basic handset, friends on campus with smartphones. The free plan covers the safety basics, your circle, alarms and trips, so a student budget is never the reason to go without.

  • Up to seven people in your circle
  • Works on any phone, smartphone or not
  • Safety basics are free on the Basic plan
  • Everyone can reach you and be reached
Share a trip

The late journey, watched to the gate.

The night library, a late lecture, the journey between home and school: start a watch and your circle follows along on its own. If you stop or go quiet, they are told where you were last seen, so no one is guessing whether you made it back.

  • Automatic check-ins as you travel
  • Last-seen alert if you go quiet
  • Start a watch from WhatsApp in seconds
  • Close it quietly when you arrive
Know your area

Learn a new town before you explore it.

Arriving somewhere unfamiliar, the live map shows concerns reported nearby and a Risk Score for an area, so you learn which routes and times are calmer before you are out in them.

  • A live map of concerns reported nearby
  • A quick Risk Score for any area
  • Follow your hall, your route, your campus
  • Flag a concern for others, without your name
