Building a circle for students far from home

5 min read

Being far from family does not mean being out of reach. A student circle mixes the people around you now with the people who have always had your back.

Add the people who are actually near

Roommates, close friends on campus, someone a few minutes away. When you raise an alarm, FlagRisk reaches the members near enough to help, so a couple of nearby people make the circle real.

Keep family in the circle too

Parents and siblings far away still belong in it. They may not reach you first, but they are the people who will always answer, and they can call for help toward you.

Use Trip Watch for the ordinary nights

Late library sessions, a long walk back, a trip to a new part of the city. Share the trip and let it check you in, so a quiet night does not go unnoticed.

Learn a new city before it surprises you

Check the map and the Risk Score when you arrive somewhere new, and follow the places you spend time in, so you hear when a concern is reported nearby.
