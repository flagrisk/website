Share a trip

Trip Watch: share a journey, arrive watched.

Before you set out, say where you are going and who is watching. Trip Watch follows the journey with you, so you do not have to watch it yourself.

Create your accountUse on WhatsApp

Location sharing for safety should not mean watching a map the whole way home. Trip Watch does the watching for you. You name a destination and your watchers, then travel as normal; it checks you in on its own and raises the alarm only if something is wrong.

How Trip Watch works

A journey that watches itself.

It checks in for you

Carry your phone as usual. Trip Watch checks you in along the way on its own and closes quietly when you arrive. There is nothing to remember to press.

If something changes

If you go silent or run past your time, your circle is told where you were last seen, without you lifting a finger.

Start it anywhere

Begin a timed watch from the app or from a WhatsApp chat, with nothing to install, so it fits whatever you are carrying.
