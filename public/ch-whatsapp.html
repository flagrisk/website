On WhatsApp

FlagRisk safety, right inside WhatsApp.

You can use FlagRisk from a WhatsApp chat, with nothing to install. For many people, the safest thing to carry is the app they already open every day.

Create your accountUse on WhatsApp

A safety app on WhatsApp removes the biggest barrier, which is getting people to install one more thing. FlagRisk works inside the chat your circle already uses. From a message you can raise an alarm, run a timed watch, check an area and bring people in.
