Know your area

Read the area before you step into it.

FlagRisk puts the safety concerns reported near you onto one map, so you can choose a safer route or a safer time before you are out in it.

Create your accountUse on WhatsApp
One map

Concerns nearby, with a Risk Score.

The map draws safety concerns reported near you, from the people around you and from published news, in one place, with a quick Risk Score for any area so you can read it at a glance.

  • A live map of concerns reported nearby
  • A quick Risk Score for any area
  • Reports from people and from the news
  • Read an area before you go there
The places that matter

Follow a place, hear of concerns near it.

Follow your home, your route, a child's school, and FlagRisk tells you when a concern is reported near one of them. When you flag a concern yourself, it shows on the map without your name, so other people can act on it too.

  • Follow home, route or a school
  • Told when a concern is reported near them
  • Flag a concern, shown without your name
  • Helps others choose a safer route
