Know your area

A live risk map to know your area.

FlagRisk puts the safety concerns reported near you onto one map, so you can choose a safer route or a safer time before you step into it.

Create your accountUse on WhatsApp

Knowing which areas to avoid used to be word of mouth. The FlagRisk risk map makes it something you can check. It draws safety concerns reported near you, from the people around you and from published news, onto one map, with a Risk Score for any area.

What the map shows

The area, read at a glance.

Concerns reported nearby

See safety concerns reported close to you, from people in the area and from the news, in one place rather than scattered across rumour.

A Risk Score for any area

A quick score tells you how an area is reading right now, so you can weigh a route or a time at a glance.

Follow the places that matter

Follow your home, your route or a school, and hear when a concern is reported near it. Flag a concern yourself and it shows without your name.

Read the area before you set out.

Used before a journey, the map helps you pick a safer way home; used day to day, it keeps you aware of the places you and your family move through.
