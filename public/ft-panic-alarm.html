Raise an alarm

A silent panic alarm and SOS.

When something feels wrong, one press tells the people who can reach you where you are, without a sound and without showing anything on your screen.

Create your accountUse on WhatsApp

A panic button app only helps if it works in the moment it is needed, quietly, in a vehicle or a room with the threat still in it. FlagRisk is built for that. The alarm makes no sound, shows nothing on screen, and sends your live location to the people in your circle who are near enough to come.

How the alarm works

Help, without being seen to ask.

One silent press

No sound, nothing on screen. The alarm sends your live location the instant it fires, so you can call for help without being seen to.

They know you need them

The people in your circle can mark that they have seen the alarm, so you know someone is on the way, not just a notification into the dark.

Across every channel

Raise an alarm from the app, from WhatsApp, by SMS, or with a USSD code, so the phone in your hand is always enough.

When it matters

Backup that still gets through.

If the people you need cannot be reached by a notification, a paid plan reaches them by SMS, so the alarm does not depend on everyone having data at that moment.
