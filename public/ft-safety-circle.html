Your circle

Your safety circle: the people you trust.

FlagRisk is built on the people you trust, not strangers. Your circle is who an alarm reaches and who watches a trip, so the first responders are the people who love you.

Create your accountUse on WhatsApp

A safety circle is the heart of FlagRisk. Instead of a call centre, it puts the people who can actually reach you one tap away: family, close friends, a neighbour. They receive your alarms, they watch your trips, and they can be on any phone.

How your circle works

The people who show up.

Up to seven you trust

Add family and friends whatever phone they carry, from a smartphone to a basic handset on USSD. Everyone can reach you and be reached.

On your terms

Your circle sees what you share, an alarm or a trip you started, not a constant track of where you are.

Easy to build

Bring people in from a WhatsApp chat, so setting up a circle takes minutes, and add or remove people whenever you want.

Not strangers

Help that is never a stranger.

When an alarm goes out, it goes to the people who know you and know where you are likely to be, which is what makes it fast.
