For women

A personal safety app for women.

On the commute, in a taxi, walking home after dark: FlagRisk keeps the people you trust one tap away and watches the journey, so you are never really on your own.

Create your accountUse on WhatsApp

A personal safety app for women has to work in the real moments, when you cannot be seen reaching for help and there is no time to explain. FlagRisk is built for those moments in Nigeria. You raise a silent alarm to the people you trust, share a ride home so someone follows it, and read an area before you walk into it. Nothing about it announces itself.

How it helps

Safety that fits the real moments.

Raise a silent alarm

A panic button for women that makes no sound and shows nothing on screen. One press sends your live location to the people in your circle near enough to reach you, and you see who is on the way.

Share the ride home

Share a taxi, an okada or the walk home with Trip Watch. It checks you in on its own, and if you go quiet or arrive late, your circle is told where you were last seen.

Know the area first

Read the live risk map before you set out, with a Risk Score for any area, so you can pick a safer route or a safer time.

Built for Nigeria

The people who love you, a tap away.

FlagRisk reaches your circle on whatever phone they carry, through the app, WhatsApp, SMS and USSD. On a paid plan, anyone who cannot be reached by a notification is reached by text, so the alarm still gets through.
