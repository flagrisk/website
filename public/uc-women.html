A personal safety app built around the people you trust.

The commute, the ride home, meeting someone new: FlagRisk keeps the people you trust one tap away, and watches the journey so you do not have to.

Raise an alarm

A panic button that stays silent.

Some moments do not allow you to be seen reaching for help, in a vehicle or a room with the threat still in it. One press sends your live location to the people in your circle near enough to reach you. It makes no sound and shows nothing on your screen, and you see who has marked that they are on the way.

  • Silent: no sound, nothing on screen
  • Live location to your circle the moment it fires
  • You see who has acknowledged
  • SMS backup to your circle on a paid plan
Share a trip

Share the ride home, arrive watched.

Before the taxi, the okada or the walk home, say where you are going and who is watching. FlagRisk checks you in along the way on its own. If you arrive, it closes quietly. If you go silent or do not get there in time, your circle is told where you were last seen, without you lifting a finger.

  • Set your destination and who watches
  • Automatic check-ins as you travel
  • Last-seen alert if you go quiet
  • Start it from the app or from WhatsApp
Know your area

Read a place before you step into it.

FlagRisk draws safety concerns reported nearby, from people around you and from published news, onto one map, with a quick Risk Score for any area. When you flag a concern yourself, it shows without your name, so other women can choose a safer route or a safer time.

  • A live map of concerns reported nearby
  • A quick Risk Score for any area
  • Follow a place, hear of concerns near it
  • Flag a concern, shown without your name
