For travel

Every journey, watched to the door.

The daily commute, the intercity road trip, the late ride home. FlagRisk follows the journey with you and reaches your circle the moment something changes.

Create your accountUse on WhatsApp
Share a trip

Set out, and let the journey watch itself.

Say where you are going and who is watching, then carry your phone as you normally would. FlagRisk checks you in along the way on its own. If you arrive, it closes. If you go quiet or run late, your circle is told where you were last seen.

  • Set your destination and who watches
  • Automatic check-ins as you travel
  • Last-seen alert if you go quiet or late
  • Start it from the app or from WhatsApp
Know your area

Read the route before you take it.

The live map shows concerns reported along the way and a Risk Score for an area, so you can choose a safer route or a safer time before you set out on a long road.

  • A live map of concerns reported nearby
  • A quick Risk Score for any area
  • Follow a route and hear of concerns near it
  • Flag a concern for other travellers, anonymously
Wider reach

Cover that matches the distance.

An alarm reaches the people in your circle near enough to act, and a paid plan widens how far that reach travels: fifty kilometres on Pro, five hundred on Premium, for the long journeys where the people who love you are far behind you.

  • Alarm with live location in one press
  • SMS backup to your circle on a paid plan
  • Pro reaches up to 50 km, Premium up to 500 km
  • Longer history kept on higher plans
